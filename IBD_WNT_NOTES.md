# ANTXR2 x Wnt co-expression across healthy and inflamed colon

Arm status: **exploratory, negative.** The headline is that the state-dependent
rewiring this arm was built to look for is not there once donor coverage is
controlled. Read the Corrections section before quoting anything above it.

## Data

Broad Single Cell Portal **SCP259** (Smillie et al., *Cell* 2019), ulcerative
colitis. 365,492 cells / 30 subjects / 3 clinical states (Healthy 110,110;
Non-inflamed 130,263; Inflamed 125,119) / 2 locations (LP, Epi) / 51 clusters.

Downloaded from the portal's own signed URLs. **The HCA DCP mirror of this
study serves truncated Epi and Fib matrices** — the copies used here were
checked against their companion files before any analysis:

| compartment | matrix | genes.tsv | barcodes2.tsv | last entry in bounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Epi | 20028 x 123006, 174,423,911 nnz | 20028 | 123006 | yes |
| Fib | 19076 x 31872, 39,087,919 nnz | 19076 | 31872 | yes |
| Imm | 20529 x 210614, 173,255,714 nnz | 20529 | 210614 | yes |

`build_scp259_h5ad.py` encodes this as a hard failure, not a warning. A
completed download is not evidence of correct content.

## Method

memento point-estimate correlations between *ANTXR2* and `GENE_PANEL_ARMS`
`WNT_ARM`, computed per **donor x cell type x state**, then averaged across
donors with **equal weight per donor**.

Two things to know about the implementation:

- memento's `get_2d_moments(groupby=...)` averages groups **cell-count
  weighted**, which violates the project's donor-equal-weight convention.
  `wnt_state_coexpression.py` therefore takes the raw per-group columns and
  averages them unweighted itself.
- memento's expression filter drops a *different* subset of Wnt genes in each
  state. Contrasting those would compare a correlation against a gene that
  merely failed the filter, so contrasts are restricted to pairs present in
  both arms; filter-asymmetric pairs go to `wnt_state_asymmetric.csv` rather
  than being silently dropped.

**Capture rate `q` = 0.1** (`config.DEFAULT_CAPTURE_RATE`). SCP259 is 10x 3' v2
and ships no per-library sequencing-saturation metric, so the empirical route in
`capture_rate.py` could not be applied. This is an assumption. Absolute mean
estimates depend on it; correlation estimates are far less sensitive.

## What the unfiltered ranking looked like

Ranking all 87 epithelial contrasts by magnitude produced a clean story:
receptor and transduction components (*LRP5*, *TCF7L2*, *FZD5*, *CTNNB1*)
becoming **more** co-expressed with *ANTXR2* under inflammation, and negative
regulators (*ZNRF3*, *RNF43*, *AXIN2*) moving the other way. Largest single
shift: Secretory TA x *LRP5*, -0.089 -> +0.653.

## Corrections — this supersedes the section above

That story is an artifact of donor scarcity.

- Secretory TA x *LRP5* rests on **3 inflamed donors**. So do most of the
  extreme shifts.
- Restricted to pairs with **>=8 donors in both arms** (43 pairs, 6 cell types,
  8 genes): median change **-0.000**, **21 up / 22 down**, Wilcoxon signed-rank
  **p = 0.77**.
- |change| is strongly inversely related to donor count across all 87 pairs:
  Spearman **rho = -0.479, p = 2.7e-06**. Median |change| falls 0.209 (3-4
  donors) -> 0.150 (5-7) -> 0.103 (>=8).

**Conclusion: no detectable state-dependent change in *ANTXR2*-Wnt
co-expression in colonic epithelium.** This is the same failure mode as the
withdrawn organ-involvement enrichment in `HFS_ATLAS_NOTES.md` — a striking
ordering generated by selecting on the noisiest cells of the design.

## Why the low-donor contrasts move: estimator saturation, not donor biology

The mechanism is degenerate per-donor estimates, not three donors carrying a
real signal. Of 3,749 donor-level estimates, **358 (9.5%) are pegged at
|r| > 0.99** — essentially exactly +/-1 — and a further **19.2% return no
estimate at all**. Both pathologies are driven by cells per donor x cell type:

| cells per donor x cell type | n | pegged at abs(r)>0.99 | no estimate | SD across donors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <100 | 971 | 14.1% | 41.8% | 0.611 |
| 100-250 | 1321 | 11.7% | 19.0% | 0.518 |
| 250-500 | 930 | 4.8% | 6.1% | 0.371 |
| 500-1k | 383 | 5.2% | 1.3% | 0.340 |
| >1k | 144 | 1.4% | 1.4% | 0.211 |

The headline contrast is the clean worked example. **Secretory TA x *LRP5*,
inflamed** had 6 donors clearing the 50-cell floor; 3 returned no estimate, and
of the 3 that remained, **two are exactly +1.000** (N9, 67 cells; N23, 62
cells) and one is -0.042. The inflamed "correlation" of +0.653 is
`mean(-0.042, 1.000, 1.000)`. The healthy arm has the same pathology — N46 at
-1.000, N16 at +1.000, N17 at 0.904 — but 11 donors dilute it to -0.089.

So the ceiling hits are **not** confined to the low-donor strata: 78 of 87
contrasts contain at least one, at similar rates in both (88.6% of <8-donor
contrasts, 90.7% of >=8-donor contrasts). What differs is the denominator. One
pegged donor moves a 3-donor mean by 0.33 and an 11-donor mean by 0.09. The
donors are not special; the averaging is.

**Robustness.** Dropping every pegged estimate and re-requiring >=8 donors in
both arms leaves 36 pairs: median change **-0.028**, **13 up / 23 down**,
Wilcoxon **p = 0.18**. Still null, and the residual tilt is slightly *downward* —
the opposite direction to the discarded "receptor arm goes up" story.

**Practical consequence for this project:** a >=50-cell floor per donor x cell
type is too permissive for memento correlations. On this data the pegged and
no-estimate rates only fall below ~5% above roughly 250 cells per group.

## Is there a better-powered cell type? No — the ceiling is ANTXR2 detection

Asked whether some larger cell type would give robust estimates. It would not,
and the reason is not cell numbers.

**Gate 1, cells.** At >=250 cells per donor x cell type, only 2 of 33 clusters
have >=8 donors present in both paired states (Plasma 14, TA 1 8). At >=500
cells, one (Plasma).

**Gate 2, ANTXR2 detection — this is the binding one.** ANTXR2 is detected in
1-11% of cells in every large cluster: Plasma 2.2%, TA 1 1.1%, CD4+ Memory
1.9%, Cycling TA 7.8%. The clusters with real detection are all small:
Inflammatory Fibroblasts 25.8% (2,268 cells), Enterocytes 21.7% (4,517),
WNT2B+ Fos-lo 1 19.6% (6,350), Best4+ Enterocytes 17.6% (3,286). Nothing is
both big and ANTXR2-positive.

The effective sample size is therefore **ANTXR2+ cells per donor**, not cells.
Combined estimator failure (pegged at |r|=1 **or** no estimate returned) falls
monotonically against it:

| ANTXR2+ cells per donor x cell type | n | estimates that fail |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | 1080 | 41.9% |
| 6-10 | 714 | 33.1% |
| 11-20 | 894 | 26.6% |
| 21-50 | 626 | 17.7% |
| >50 | 435 | 9.7% |

Note the pegged rate *alone* is non-monotone (6.9% in the 0-5 bin, peaking at
13.4% in 6-10): the very sparsest groups return NaN instead of a pegged value.
The two failure modes must be counted together — Spearman of pegged-vs-ANTXR2+
alone is rho = 0.003, p = 0.83, which would be misread as "no relationship".

**At the >=50 ANTXR2+ / >=8 paired donors target, zero groups qualify.** Best
cell type is 2 donors; pooling to lineage gets fibroblasts to 5, TA/stem and
enterocyte lineage to 4. Pooling does not rescue it.

SCP259 cannot support a donor-level ANTXR2 co-expression contrast at any
granularity. This is a property of ANTXR2's detection rate in 10x 3' v2, not of
the analysis choices — so it also bounds the non-inflamed arm that was proposed
as the next step.

**Design note discovered here:** the 30 subjects are 12 healthy controls plus
18 UC patients, and all 18 patients contributed *both* an inflamed and a
non-inflamed biopsy. The healthy-vs-inflamed contrast reported above is
therefore unpaired (different people); non-inflamed-vs-inflamed would be paired
within patient. That is the better design, but it hits the same ANTXR2 ceiling.

## Caveats

- **Exploratory.** Positive correlations here have *not* passed
  `anchor_gene_control.py`. That control's outcome differs by cell type and
  must not be applied uniformly.
- Anti-correlations remain the stronger result class (shared activation can
  manufacture co-expression but not mutual exclusivity); none of the
  anti-correlations here survive the donor-coverage filter as state-dependent.
- *AXIN2* is grouped above with the destruction complex, but it is equally a
  canonical Wnt **target** and so reads partly as an activation readout. The
  grouping is stated rather than forced; nothing in the conclusion depends on it.
- The stromal compartment yields only 15 contrasts across 3 genes — most Wnt
  receptor/target genes fall below the expression filter in stroma, as expected.
  It is reported for completeness, not as an independent test.
- Non-inflamed tissue is computed and saved but not contrasted here.
- Single study, single tissue, 10x 3' v2 only. No replication cohort.
