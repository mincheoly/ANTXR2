# ANTXR2 regulation and activity inference using single cell genomics

## One-line version
Study the cell type specific regulation and downstream pathways of ANTXR2 gene, particularly in context of hyaline familial fibromatosis, not just in the skin but also in the gut and immune system. 

## Motivation
Medical genetics often involve monogenic disorders. For example, Dr. Shieh prompted me to study existing datasets and literature for the effect of ANTXR2 mutation, classically causing hyaline fibromatosis syndrome. His only real direction was "why don't you consider the coexpression of this gene?"

There is a secondary motivation of further developing the workflow of using tools like memento (and many others) when there is a specific gene is of interest, rather than looking at broad effects of perturbations, particularly using single cell data.

## Current thoughts

1) Cell-type specific expression and coexpression
2) Predicted pathway analysis of how the gene itself is regulated
3) The predicted effect of perturbing this gene, based on CRISPR datasets as well as more up-and-coming predictive models.

## Failure modes
At the end of the day, it would rely on existing data. But for the scope of this kind of work (pipeline/approach to studying single genes in the context of transcriptomics), that might be okay.
